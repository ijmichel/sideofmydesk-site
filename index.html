What do I offer?

I can automate most of the routine work in a parish office: the copying, pasting and checking that someone does every week, usually alone. Every parish runs its office differently, so each setup is built around your own templates, schedule and accounts. You pick the pieces you want.

  • The prayer list

    Requests collected in one place, names added only with permission, and older entries aged off on the schedule you choose.

  • The Sunday handout

    This week’s readings, hymns, notices and prayers merged into your own handout layout, in regular and large print.

  • The service email

    The same details sent to the parish list as a clean weekly email, drafted from the handout so the two always match.

  • The recording link

    Last Sunday’s Zoom or livestream recording trimmed, posted and linked in the next email.

  • The vestry memo

    A short monthly finance summary drafted from your bookkeeping reports, for the treasurer to check.

  • Your other routines

    Newsletters, calendar notices, volunteer reminders and other repeat tasks, set up to match how your parish already does them.

How a week runs

Your office keeps doing what it does now. The drafting moves off its plate.

  1. Through Wednesday

    Requests, notices and hymn choices come in the way they do today: email, a form or a note from the rector.

  2. Thursday by noon

    Drafts of the prayer list, the handout and the service email are ready in your office account.

  3. Thursday or Friday

    Your administrator reviews them, makes any changes and sends or prints. The goal is about 15 minutes.

  4. Sunday

    The handout is in the pews, and the email and recording link are already out.

Careful with what people share

A prayer list holds things people told the church in trust. It is set up with that in mind from the first day.

  • Drafts onlyNothing is sent, printed or posted until someone at the church approves it.
  • Names with permissionHealth details stay general, children are not named, and names leave the list on schedule.
  • Your accounts, your filesIt works in the church’s own email and documents, with access limited to what the job needs.
